**Dear Bestie,** Konni friendships ela start ayyayo manaki kuda sarigga gurthu undadu… Kaani avi eppudu mana life lo oka important part ayipoyayo matram teliyakundane jarigipothundi. Nuvvu kuda alanti person❤️. Mana madhya unna bond ni oka word lo explain cheyyadam kastam. Friends ani cheppadam chala simple… kaani nuvvu naaku just “friend” kante ekkuva. Natho navvinappudu, na silly talks vini irritate ayinappudu, nannu tease chesinappudu, nenu silent ga unna kuda em jarigindo ardham chesukunnappudu— aa small moments anni naaku chala special🥰. Life lo chala mandi vastaru. Konni friendships konni rojulu untayi, konni konni years untayi. Kaani konthamandi matram mana memories lo permanent place tiskuntaru. **Nuvvu alanti person.** Mana madhya fights untayi, arguments untayi, misunderstandings kuda ravachu. Kaani avi mana friendship ni marchipoye reason kaavu. Endukante mana bond lo perfect moments kanna, **real moments ekkuva important.** Nenu eppudu perfect friend kaakapovachu. Konni saarlu ninnu irritate chesi undochu, konni saarlu ardham chesukokunda behave chesi undochu. But one thing I want you to know— **I genuinely value you.** Mana memories lo konni chala ordinary moments laga anipinchachu… kaani future lo venakki tirigi chusthe, avi mana life lo best moments ani ardham avuthayi. Mana jokes, random conversations, stupid fights, unexpected laughs, silent moments… anni kalisi mana story ni create chesayi. And honestly, **I wouldn't want to change that story.** Future lo mana paths different ayina, different places lo unna, different people mana life lo vachina… I hope mana friendship matram alage untundi. Because some people are not just a part of a particular phase of our life. **They become a part of who we are.** Nuvvu na life lo alanti oka person. Thank you for being there. Thank you for all the memories. Thank you for being exactly who you are. And most importantly… **Thank you for being my best friend.** Whatever happens next, whatever changes with time, I hope whenever you think about me, you remember all the crazy, stupid, beautiful moments we shared and smile. **Some friendships are made by time.** **Some are made by memories.** **Ours was made by both.** Always stay the same crazy person. **Yours,** **Your comrade ❤️**
